Title: I built a real-time 1v1 competitive coding platform so my friends and I could duel on DSA problems (Piston + WebSockets)

Hey everyone!

Like many CS students, my friends and I spent hundreds of hours grinding LeetCode alone preparing for technical interviews. But we realized we learned 10x faster when we raced against each other in real-time.

So over the last few months, we built AlgoFight (https://algo.mitsgwalior.in/) — an open-source real-time 1v1 coding battle arena.

Key technical stack:
- Frontend: React 19 + Monaco Editor + Tailwind + Stealth Obsidian aesthetic
- Backend: Node.js + Express + Socket.IO for sub-20ms bi-directional synchronization
- Sandboxed Execution: Piston engine running multi-container isolated environments
- Matchmaking & Caching: Redis sorted sets for Elo-based pairing
- Anti-Cheat: Focus detection and window-blur telemetry to ensure fair play

We just put together a full cinematic walkthrough of the platform. Would love your feedback and thoughts!

Demo & Arena: https://algo.mitsgwalior.in/
